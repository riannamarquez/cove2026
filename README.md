# COVE

## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

An iOS app that helps users recover from injury or maintain an active recovery routine. Users describe their symptoms through a short intake flow, and the app generates a personalized three-exercise plan. For any exercise, users can activate a real-time coaching tab that uses their phone camera and GPT-4o's VLM to analyze their form and deliver plain-English feedback, as if the user has a physical therapist giving them advice. A built-in progress log tracks completed sessions, pain notes, and streaks to keep users consistent.

## VISION

Problem: 
People recovering from injuries or wanting to maintain a recovery routine face two challenges. First, receiving updated exercise guidance requires repeated clinic visits. Clinic visits are costly, time-consuming, and often impractical. Second, when patients do their assigned exercises at home, they have no way of knowing whether they're performing them correctly, lessening the effectiveness of these exercises.

Our Vision: 
Our app gives users a personalized exercise plan on demand, generated from a short intake flow without requiring a clinic visit. (Disclaimer: We are not medical professionals so this does not entirely replace clinical visits. It’s just a supplement in between those clinical visits.) For every exercise, a real-time form coaching tab uses the phone camera and GPT-4o's vision capability to analyze movement and give plain-English feedback, so users know whether they are doing the exercises correctly.

Our app is not a replacement for professional physical therapy. It is additional support that makes professional guidance actually stick. We bridge the gap between clinic visits and keep users consistent and informed at home.
